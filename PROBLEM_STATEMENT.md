# Problem Statement - Hotel Booking Management System

## Option 1: Concise Version (Recommended for PPT)

**Problem Statement:**

Traditional hotel booking processes face several challenges:
- Manual booking processes are time-consuming and error-prone
- Lack of centralized room management system
- Difficulty in tracking bookings, revenue, and occupancy
- No automated booking confirmation system
- Inefficient customer experience with phone/email bookings
- Limited visibility into real-time room availability

---

## Option 2: Detailed Version

**Current Challenges in Hotel Booking Management:**

1. **Manual Processes**: Hotels rely on phone calls, emails, or walk-ins for bookings, leading to:
   - Time-consuming reservation handling
   - Human errors in booking details
   - Difficulty managing multiple bookings simultaneously

2. **Lack of Centralized System**: 
   - No unified platform to manage rooms and bookings
   - Difficulty tracking room availability in real-time
   - Inefficient inventory management

3. **Poor Customer Experience**:
   - Customers cannot check availability instantly
   - No online booking option
   - Long waiting times for confirmation
   - No booking history or receipt management

4. **Administrative Challenges**:
   - Difficulty tracking revenue and occupancy rates
   - Manual calculation of booking statistics
   - No automated reporting system
   - Inefficient room status management

5. **Security Concerns**:
   - No secure authentication system
   - Risk of data breaches with manual records
   - No role-based access control

## Option 4: Story-Based Approach

**The Problem:**

Hotels struggle with outdated booking methods that create inefficiencies for both customers and staff. Customers face long wait times and uncertainty about room availability, while hotel administrators spend excessive time on manual booking management, leading to errors and lost revenue opportunities.

**Key Issues:**
- Customers cannot book rooms online 24/7
- Hotel staff waste time on phone bookings
- No real-time visibility into room availability
- Manual tracking of bookings and revenue
- Risk of double bookings and errors
- No automated confirmation system

---

## Option 5: Technical Problem Statement

**Technical Challenges:**

1. **No Digital Platform**: Hotels lack a web-based booking system
2. **Manual Data Management**: Bookings stored in spreadsheets or paper records
3. **No API Integration**: Cannot integrate with other systems
4. **Limited Scalability**: Manual processes don't scale with business growth
5. **Data Security**: No secure way to handle customer information
6. **No Automation**: Everything requires manual intervention
